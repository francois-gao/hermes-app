Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Hermes is a private, non-commercial application used by a single individual to access and organize their own data, including data from their own Google account.

  • Access is limited to the account owner's own Google data (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive), granted through Google's standard OAuth consent process.
  • Data is used solely to provide the owner personal organization features (summaries, calendar management, reminders).
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties. No advertising or tracking is used.
  • Hermes' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The owner can revoke access at any time from their Google Account permissions page.
